# Create your class group and enrol students
Fiona MacNeill | 14 steps | 2 minutes<br>
8 June 2026<br>
#### 1. After logging into Kita, click on "Students"<br> 
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol001.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 2. Click on "Classes & Groups"<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol002.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 3. Click on "Create" on the top-right. <br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol003.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 4. Click on "Create Group"<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol004.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 5. Type in a name for your class group and select “Save”.<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol005.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 6. Click on the three dots action menu on the right-hand side of your group<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol006.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 7. Click on "Manage enrolments"<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol007.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 8. Click on "+" next to the courses you want to add.<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol008.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 9. You can select multiple courses at the same time. <br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol009.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 10. Click on "Save”.<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol010.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 11. Click on "Confirm Enrolment" to confirm the list of courses<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol011.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 12. Click on your Join Group Link<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol012.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 13. Select the copy link button<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol013.webp" width="90%" /><br>
#### 14. Close the code window and share the code with your students<br>
<img src="/images/001classenrol/enrol014.webp" width="90%" /><br>
