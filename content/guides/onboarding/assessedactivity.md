# Create a assessed activity in Kita
Fiona MacNeill | 36 steps | 10 minutes<br>
15 June 2026<br>
This tutorial explain how to create your assessed activity. This allows you to accept submissions for an activity and use the marking and assessment tools available in Kita. <br>
#### 1. Select the course where you would like to create the assignment.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep002.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 2. Select the lesson. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep003.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 3. Click on the Edit instructions button.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep004.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 4. Scroll down to where the spot where you would like the assessed activity. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep005.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 5. The Evaluate section of a Time2Code lesson is a good spot after the expected outputs. Put your cursor where you would like the assignment. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep006.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 6. Click on "+ Add assessment" in the tool palette.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep007.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 7. Click on Assessed Activity.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep008.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 8. The title and description are picked up automatically and may not need to be edited. We recommend adding your success criteria or learning outcomes/objectives. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep009.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 9. Use the text edit tools to style your objectives as needed. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep010.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 10. Click on the Show Due Date to Students toggle to enable a due date.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep011.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 11. Click on the date field to set the due date. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep012.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 12. Select the due date from the calendar. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep013.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 13. Then specify the time.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep014.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 14. Be sure to specify if the due date is in the morning \(AM\) or afternoon \(PM\). <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep015.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 15. Click on "Expected filenames" and type in the name of the file you expect. <br>To note main.py is typical for Time2Code and you can ask for multiple files. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep016.webp" width="90%"><br>
As additional note here. These filenames are a guide to help you keep things organised. If students submit a file with a different filename it will still be accepted. Also if students submit the wrong file, they can submit another alternative file. You can also ‘unsubmit’ to return a file back to a student. <br>
#### 16. To set requirements related to the input/output tests select the number of tests and decide if they need to be run or passed prior to submission.  <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep018.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 17. In my example, I’ve decided that they just need to be run and not passed before the student submits.  <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep019.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 18. If you want to use a quantitative rubric or to provide a score add maximum possible score for the activity. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep020.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 19. In my example each my three criteria are worth 9 points, so I am marking out of 27. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep021.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 20. Click on the rubric option: ”Quantitative \(with marks\)"<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep022.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 21. Add the number of Criteria - in my example I have three. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep023.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 22. Then add the number of levels in your scale, e.g., A, B, C, D, E would be 5 levels. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep024.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 23. Paste in your scale from lowest to highest. In my example I’m using: <br>Incomplete, Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Mastery. <br>Inc<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep025.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 24. I’ve completed the whole row and will now move on to the criteria. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep026.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 25. I have a title and description for each criterion. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep027.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 26. Once I’ve added my criteria, observe that I need to add points to my rubric to add up to the total based on my maximum score. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep028.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 27. Add your points along your points along the top to specific the same points for all criteria. To weight scores, add different points per criterion row. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep029.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 28. Add in your points, I find it easiest to go from right to left \(highest to lowest\). <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep030.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 29. Once you have scales and criteria, you can then generate each row of descriptors using AI.  <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep031.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 30. Finish generating all your rows and scroll to the bottom of the screen. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep032.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 31. Double-check that all the points are distributed. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep033.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 32. If you need to make this assignment ready later, select Save as draft. Otherwise select Publish to make it available to students immediately. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep034.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 33. Click on Save to save your changes in the editor. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep035.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 34. Navigate to the Evaluate section to see the assessment information as students see it. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep036.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 35. Here it is right after Evaluate. <br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep038.webp" width="90%"><br>
#### 36. Select the Edit assessment button to get back to the settings.<br>
<img src="/images/002assess/AssessmentStep039.webp" width="90%"><br>
