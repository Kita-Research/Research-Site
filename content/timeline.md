# Timeline

<br /> 

## Kita pilot — teacher task schedule

All tasks below are for the attention of the teacher or computing lead at your school. Tasks marked **case study schools only** apply to a subset of participating schools.

--- 
&nbsp;

## Week of 8 June

### Access your school's Kita instance

You should have received a link to your school's Kita instance by now. If you haven't received it, please let us know as soon as possible so we can get this to you.

--- 
&nbsp;

## Week of 15 June

### Set up your school instance

- Add your students and class groups to your school's Kita instance.
- Choose one lesson to be formally assessed during the pilot.
  - *Once you've chosen your lesson, you'll set up the assessed activity in Kita — we're happy to help with this if you'd like support.*
- Complete the pre-pilot survey (includes the research study consent section).

--- 
&nbsp;

## Week of 22 June

### First lesson & assessment setup

- Run your first lesson using Kita with students.
  - *This session is about familiarisation — helping students get comfortable with the platform before the assessed lesson.*
- Confirm your assessed activity is set up in Kita and ready to run next week.
- **Case study schools only:** Collect signed parent and student consent forms for the case study.

--- 
&nbsp;

## Week of 29 June

### Assessed lesson & post-activity surveys

- Run the assessed activity with your students in Kita.
- Ensure post-activity surveys are completed by students (and yourself, if applicable).
- **Case study schools only:** Case study interview takes place this week.

--- 
&nbsp;

## Week of 6 July

### Case study interviews

- **Case study schools only:** Case study interviews continue this week if not completed in the week of 29 June.
